MusicHoarder is the app for your own music collection. It plays the library on your MusicHoarder server, the free and open-source app that identifies, tags and organises your music files, so the songs you own come with you on your phone.

You need a MusicHoarder server to use the app. Enter its address on the first screen and sign in. Want to look around first? Enter musichoarder.app and tap "Try the demo".

LISTEN
• Albums, artists, tracks, favourites and playlists from your own library
• Full-screen Now Playing with time-synced lyrics and music videos
• Radio that keeps playing similar songs from your library when the queue runs out
• Search across everything as you type

PLAYS LIKE A MUSIC APP SHOULD
• Keeps playing when the screen is off, and moves on to the next song by itself
• Controls on the lock screen, in the notification and from your headphones
• Lossless and high-resolution files play in their original quality
• Pick up where you left off on another device

SHARE
• Invite family and friends to their own account on your server
• Share a song or album with anyone through a link
• Chat with the people you share your music with

YOURS
• Your music and your listening history stay on your own server
• No ads, no subscription
• Free and open source under the MIT license

MusicHoarder does not sell or stream music. It plays files you already own, from a server you run.

Source code and setup guide: github.com/Jeffreyyvdb/MusicHoarder
